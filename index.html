The same country can feature in very different board games. Among 871 games linked to real places, military themes are associated with longer playing times and more complex rules.

Places

The US leads the country counts; Europe has the most cities.

BoardGameGeek, a community-maintained game catalogue, links games to countries and cities through tags. These can describe settings and other associations, rather than where games are made. A game can have several tags.

Ways to play

Military themes are more common in games tagged Germany than Japan.

The country totals conceal different kinds of games. BoardGameGeek groups games about military conflict as “Wargames”.

A theme describes what a game is about. Its mechanics describe what you do: manage cards, roll dice or collect sets.

Time and complexity

Wargames tend to take longer and have more complex rules.

Those themes also correspond to different playing demands. In this sample, the median listed time is two hours for Wargames and one hour for other games.

Player counts

Longer games do not always seat more players.

A shared place tells you little about the evening ahead. Military themes tend to come with longer play and more complex rules, but player capacity is a separate constraint. For a game between classes or after dinner, check all three: time, complexity and how many people can join.
